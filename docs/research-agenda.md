# Research agenda / 研究议程

**Status: a developing agenda, not completed findings or a publication list.**  
**状态：持续完善中的研究草案，尚不构成研究结论或发表成果。**

This workspace explores the intersection of social law and the regulation of large language models and AI systems. Its working premise is methodological: source-based legal analysis, observable system behavior, and a proposed normative position should be recorded separately. The four tools support that separation by preserving reading materials, source locations, review decisions, and research tasks.

本工作区关注社会法与大语言模型、人工智能系统规制的交叉问题。研究首先区分原始法律材料的分析、可以观察的系统行为以及拟提出的规范立场。四个工具通过保留阅读材料、来源位置、复核判断和研究任务，为这种区分提供基础。

## 1. Workers and algorithmic management / 劳动者与算法管理

**Question.** How do automated work allocation, monitoring, evaluation, and suspension affect workers' ability to understand and contest decisions? Which procedural protections can be argued for, and what evidence would be needed to assess their practical operation?

**问题。** 自动分配、监控、评价与暂停接单如何影响劳动者理解和质疑决定的能力？应当论证哪些程序保障，又需要什么材料评价这些保障的实际运行？

The proposed doctrinal work would select a jurisdiction and a defined decision type before collecting primary sources. It would distinguish employment-status questions from information access, review, remedies, and evidence issues. Sources would include applicable legislation, official decisions, and versioned platform policies where available. Their jurisdiction, date, authority, and applicability would require independent verification.

拟议的规范研究应先确定法域和具体决定类型，再收集一手材料；将劳动关系认定与信息获得、复核、救济及证据问题分别处理。可以收集适用立法、官方裁判和具有版本信息的平台规则，并独立核验法域、日期、来源地位与适用范围。

A possible empirical component would examine publicly accessible explanation and appeal workflows through a documented sampling protocol. Observations would record what a user can actually see or do, without inferring an employer's internal model or treating an interface observation as a legal conclusion. Interviews or nonpublic worker data would require a separate access and ethics design before collection.

可能的经验研究部分是按明确的抽样方案检查公开可访问的解释与申诉流程。记录使用者实际能够看到或执行的操作，不据此推断内部模型，也不将界面观察直接转化为法律结论。访谈与非公开劳动者材料，应当另行完成资料获取及研究伦理设计后再收集。

**Proposed outputs:** an annotated source map, a comparison of decision-review pathways, and a reasoned memorandum identifying both proposed protections and evidential gaps.

**拟议成果：** 带注释的来源图谱、决定复核路径比较，以及分别说明保障主张与证据缺口的研究备忘录。

## 2. Social protection and access / 社会保障与服务可及性

**Question.** How should AI-mediated benefit services be examined for accessibility, unequal burdens, and effective routes to human assistance? Where should the analysis distinguish document organization, advisory output, and a decision about eligibility?

**问题。** 如何考察 AI 参与福利服务时的无障碍程度、不均等负担与有效人工求助渠道？材料整理、咨询输出和资格决定之间，应当如何区分？

The normative component would identify a bounded service and the relevant legal materials. Any argument about appropriate safeguards would state its premises and distinguish existing rules from proposed changes. Comparisons across jurisdictions would check differences in institutions and entitlement structures before drawing an analogy.

规范研究部分应当确定范围清楚的服务场景和相关法律材料。关于保障措施的论证须说明前提，区分现行规则与拟议改革；跨法域比较应先核对制度与给付资格结构的差异，再展开类比。

The empirical component could use publicly documented service workflows or a clearly labeled prototype. Candidate observations include task completion, misunderstood instructions, access to human assistance, and differing burdens across user groups. These are proposed measurement dimensions; this workspace has not established service effects or causal estimates. A synthetic prototype would not be represented as an evaluation of a real public service.

经验研究可以使用公开记载的服务流程或明确标注性质的原型。拟观察的维度包括任务完成、指令误解、人工帮助可及性以及不同群体的负担差异。这些只是候选测量维度，本工作区尚未确立真实服务效果或因果估计。合成原型不能被表述为对真实公共服务的评价。

**Proposed outputs:** a service-stage map, an explicit set of normative evaluation criteria, and an observation protocol with limits on what its data can establish.

**拟议成果：** 服务阶段图、明确的规范评价标准，以及说明数据解释边界的观察方案。

## 3. LLM accountability in legal research / 大语言模型辅助法学研究的可问责性

**Question.** What evidence is needed to assess citation reliability, retrieval failures, and the traceability of model-assisted legal work? How can metadata agreement, relevance, and support for a particular claim be evaluated as distinct tasks?

**问题。** 评价引文可靠性、检索失败与模型辅助法学研究的可追溯性，需要什么证据？如何分别考察元数据一致、材料相关与具体论断得到支持这三类任务？

The current tool suite offers deterministic baselines: reference parsing, optional DOI metadata comparison, paragraph retrieval, and a human evidence notebook. The Evidence Atlas benchmark is an actual, reproducible test on a small synthetic collection. Its results describe that fixture alone. They are not an evaluation of a deployed LLM, a real legal database, or legal reasoning quality.

现有工具提供确定性的基础方法：书目解析、可选 DOI 元数据比较、段落检索和人工证据笔记。证据图谱的基准是在小规模合成语料上实际运行、可以复现的测试，结果仅描述该固定样本，不能评价已部署的大模型、真实法律数据库或法律推理质量。

A proposed next study would build an authorized, source-verified collection, define relevance and claim-support labels before scoring, and record disagreements between reviewers. Future model comparisons would name the model/version, prompt, sampling settings, collection version, and evaluation procedure. Retrieval failure, nonexistent reference, inaccurate metadata, unsupported proposition, and unresolved applicability would remain separate error categories.

下一步可以在有权使用、来源经过核验的真实语料上，先界定相关性和论断支持标签，再进行评分，并记录复核者之间的分歧。未来如开展模型比较，应明确模型与版本、提示词、采样设置、语料版本和评价步骤。检索失败、来源不存在、元数据错误、论断缺乏支持与适用性尚未解决，应作为不同错误类型处理。

**Proposed outputs:** a versioned evaluation set, an annotation guide, a failure taxonomy, and an evidence-linked report that includes counterexamples and unresolved cases.

**拟议成果：** 具有版本信息的评价集、标注指南、失败类型表，以及保留反例和未解决情形的证据关联报告。

## Evidence design / 证据设计

| Work component | What it can contribute | What requires additional work |
|---|---|---|
| Primary-source legal analysis / 一手法律材料分析 | An interpretation tied to identified text, jurisdiction, date, and scope / 与特定文本、法域、日期及范围关联的解释 | Applicability to a concrete dispute and defensible normative argument / 对具体争议的适用及可辩护的规范论证 |
| Documented system observation / 系统行为观察 | A record of what happened under stated conditions / 明确条件下实际发生之事的记录 | Generalization, causation, and access to hidden processes / 泛化、因果解释及内部过程资料 |
| Retrieval benchmark / 检索基准 | Ranked-passage performance against declared labels / 按明示标签评价段落排序 | Source authority, legal effect, and claim entailment / 来源权威、法律效力与论断支持 |
| Human annotation / 人工标注 | A reviewer's reasoned assessment and disagreement record / 复核者的判断理由与分歧记录 | Independent review and substantiation of the underlying proposition / 独立复核及论断本身的论证 |

The tools do not automatically connect every source to a legal conclusion. The proposed workflow is to preserve originals in the Literature Library, locate passages in Evidence Atlas, record bibliography and source review in Citation Audit, and connect work and deliverables in Research Roadmap. The Library's reading-list export can be imported into the Roadmap; other handoffs remain explicit exports and researcher decisions.

工具不会自动将每份来源转化为法律结论。拟采用的流程是：在文献库保留原件，在证据图谱定位段落，在引注核验中记录书目与来源复核，再通过研究路线图连接工作和成果。文献库的阅读清单可以导入路线图；其他衔接仍通过明确导出与研究者判断完成。

## Scope decisions still open / 尚待明确的范围

- Select the first jurisdiction and bounded institutional setting. / 确定首个法域和范围清楚的制度场景。
- Establish access to a suitable primary-source corpus and its update policy. / 确定适当一手语料的获取与更新办法。
- Decide which observations are feasible and what review or permissions they require. / 明确可行观察及其所需的审查或许可。
- Define comparison groups, labels, and a protocol before making empirical claims. / 在提出经验性结论前界定比较组、标签与研究方案。
