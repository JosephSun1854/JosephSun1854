# Interactive studio / 互动实验的方法

The studio brings public-facing, low-friction experiences alongside the source-centered research tools. It focuses on college students and early-career users. Each project has original content, explicit scoring rules, examples, backups and reproducible checks.

互动实验室面向大学生与初入职场者，以原创题目、公开规则与可分享结果降低体验门槛，同时保留与社会法、AI 治理及工作选择的联系。

| Project | Inputs and method | What the result means |
|---|---|---|
| [07 AI Workstyle](https://github.com/JosephSun1854/07-ai-workstyle) | 20 original collaboration situations, four bidirectional axes and hand-authored archetype distances | A snapshot of self-reported AI collaboration habits; not a validated personality instrument |
| [08 Career Compass](https://github.com/JosephSun1854/08-career-compass) | 30 original activities in six RIASEC categories, explicit unknowns and hand-authored career-family patterns | Interests and directions to investigate; not an official O*NET assessment or career-success prediction |
| [09 Algorithm Jury](https://github.com/JosephSun1854/09-algorithm-jury) | Nine fictional dilemmas; choices allocate attention to governance dimensions, with benefits and tradeoffs shown | Priorities for discussion; not a moral score, a diagnosis or a legal ruling |
| [10 Offer Lab](https://github.com/JosephSun1854/10-offer-lab) | User-entered net cash, time and ratings; editable weights, fixed utility anchors, constraints and scenarios | Preference-sensitive comparisons; not market pay data or a decision made for the user |

Ties, weak signals and insufficient evidence should remain visible rather than being forced into a single confident label. Shared PNG cards contain summarized, voluntary results; Offer Lab omits offer names and amounts. Detailed reports and backups can contain user inputs. Stored answers are not uploaded, and the applications make no LLM or analytics calls.

并列、无明显倾向与信息不足均须在结果中体现。竖版卡片只包含供使用者自愿分享的汇总信息，Offer 卡片额外隐藏金额和方案名称；详细报告和备份可能包含输入内容。应用不上传答案，不调用大模型，也未配置访问统计。

Optional prompts are text for the user to review and voluntarily paste into another service. They are not automatic model analyses; sharing such text is a separate user action.

Primary conceptual references: [O*NET Interest Profiler](https://www.onetcenter.org/IP.html) and [OECD AI Principles](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/ai-principles.html). Items, scenarios, career-family sketches and scoring rules are original; source references do not confer validation or endorsement. Details belong in each project's method documentation.
